ABOUT AIR BRAKES

What Are Air Brakes?

Air brakes are compressed air-powered braking systems used in heavy-duty vehicles like semi-trucks, buses, and trailers. Known for their power, consistency, and safety, these systems are essential for anyone looking to work in commercial driving or transportation.

Reliable Braking

Designed for heavy commercial vehicles operating in demanding conditions.

Commercial Standard

Required for trucks, buses, trailers and many heavy-duty vehicles.

Safe Operation

COURSE CURRICULUM

What You'll Learn

Our Manitoba Air Brake Course combines classroom learning with practical training. Topics include:

Air Brake Components

Understand the components and operation of commercial air brake systems.

Dual Air Brake Systems

Learn dual air brake systems, trailer brakes, and system operation.

System Checks

Perform air leak detection, pressure testing, and complete system inspections.

Brake Safety

Study brake lag, brake fade, emergency procedures, and safe braking practices.

Daily Inspections

Complete proper daily air brake inspections following MPI requirements.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About the Air Brake Course

How long is the Air Brake Course?

Most students complete the course in 1 to 2 days, depending on class size and scheduling.

Is the Air Brake Course required for a Class 1 licence?

Yes. It is mandatory in Manitoba for commercial drivers operating vehicles equipped with air brake systems.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just a valid driver's licence and a positive attitude. We provide all study materials, safety equipment, and hands-on instruction during the course.

What happens after I complete the course?

You'll receive a Certificate of Completion that you can take to MPI. After successfully passing the Air Brake Knowledge Test, the endorsement will be added to your licence.
