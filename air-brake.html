Course Overview

Learn the Skills Needed for Safe Air Brake Operation

Air brake systems are essential for commercial vehicles. This course provides detailed instruction on air brake components, operating principles, inspection procedures, brake adjustments, troubleshooting, and Manitoba regulations so drivers can confidently operate commercial vehicles equipped with air brakes.

Air Brake Components

Understand compressors, reservoirs, valves, brake chambers, slack adjusters, foundation brakes, and complete system operation.

Inspection Procedures

Learn proper pre-trip inspections, leak tests, pressure checks, warning devices, and daily maintenance procedures.

Safe Vehicle Operation

Develop confidence operating commercial vehicles while understanding emergency braking, system failures, and defensive driving techniques.

Course Curriculum

Comprehensive Air Brake Training

Our Manitoba-approved Air Brake Endorsement Course combines theory, practical demonstrations, inspections, and testing to ensure every student understands the complete operation of commercial air brake systems before obtaining their Q endorsement.

01

Introduction to Air Brake Systems

Learn why commercial vehicles use air brakes, understand system advantages, safety principles, and regulatory requirements throughout Manitoba.

02

Major Air Brake Components

Study compressors, governors, reservoirs, valves, brake chambers, push rods, slack adjusters, drums, discs, and foundation brakes.

03

System Operation

Understand air pressure generation, storage, distribution, brake application, release cycles, and emergency protection systems.

04

Daily Inspections

Perform complete pre-trip inspections, leakage testing, pressure build-up testing, warning device verification, and brake adjustment inspections.

05

Brake Adjustment

Learn proper inspection techniques for manual and automatic slack adjusters while recognizing unsafe brake conditions.

06

Safety Procedures

Understand emergency braking, system failures, downhill braking, parking brakes, and safe operating practices.

Training Process

Your Journey to an Air Brake Endorsement

Our structured training approach ensures every student develops the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to safely operate commercial vehicles equipped with air brake systems.

01

Classroom Learning

Begin with instructor-led lessons covering air brake theory, system components, regulations, inspection procedures, maintenance practices, and safe operating principles.

02

Practical Demonstrations

Watch detailed demonstrations on commercial vehicles to understand the operation of compressors, reservoirs, valves, brake chambers, and complete braking systems.

03

Hands-On Inspection

Perform complete pre-trip inspections including leakage tests, pressure build-up checks, warning device tests, and brake adjustment inspections.

04

Assessment & Certification

Complete the required knowledge and practical evaluations to demonstrate competency before applying for your Manitoba Air Brake (Q) endorsement.
