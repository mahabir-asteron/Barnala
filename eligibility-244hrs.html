ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

Age

Applicants must generally be at least 18 years of age to apply for a Class 1 commercial driver's licence in Manitoba.

Air Brake Endorsement

The Class 1 (244 Hours) program includes comprehensive Air Brake (Q) Endorsement training covering air brake system operation, inspections, brake adjustment procedures, safety practices, and the required written and practical examinations.

Driver's Licence

Applicants must hold a valid Manitoba Class 5 Full Driver's Licence, or an equivalent licence from another Canadian jurisdiction, before enrolling in the Class 1 (244 Hours) Driver Training Program.

Medical Fitness

Applicants must successfully complete a medical examination and meet Manitoba's medical standards for operating commercial vehicles before obtaining a Class 1 commercial driver's licence.

Language Proficiency

Applicants must be able to communicate effectively in English or French to understand classroom instruction, road signs, safety procedures, licensing requirements, and workplace communication.

Criminal Record Check

A criminal record check may be required. Certain criminal convictions may affect eligibility for commercial driving employment or Class 1 licensing depending on employer requirements.

Knowledge Test

Applicants must successfully pass the Manitoba Class 1 Knowledge Test covering commercial vehicle regulations, traffic laws, safe driving practices, inspections, and commercial vehicle operation before taking the practical road test.
