ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

Air Brake Course Eligibility

Age

You must meet Manitoba Public Insurance (MPI) age requirements to obtain an Air Brake (S) Endorsement.

Medical Fitness

Applicants should be medically fit to safely operate commercial vehicles equipped with air brake systems.

Knowledge Test

Students are required to successfully complete the MPI Air Brake Knowledge Test to receive the Air Brake (S) Endorsement.
