ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

Air Brake Course Eligibility

Age

You must meet Manitoba Public Insurance (MPI) age requirements to obtain an Air Brake (S) Endorsement.

Medical Fitness

You must meet the medical fitness requirements established for operating commercial vehicles equipped with air brake systems.

Knowledge Test

You must successfully complete the MPI Air Brake Knowledge Test to receive your Air Brake (S) Endorsement.
