ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

Age

Applicants must be at least 16 years of age to apply for a Manitoba Class 5 Driver's Licence. Applicants under 18 years of age require parental or guardian consent.

Knowledge Test

Applicants must successfully pass the Manitoba Class 5 Knowledge Test covering traffic signs, road rules, safe driving practices, and basic vehicle operation before receiving a learner's licence.
