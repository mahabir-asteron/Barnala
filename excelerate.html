What is ExceleRATE?

ExceleRATE is a proactive driver risk evaluation designed to answer the question of driver fitness-for-duty.


How does ExceleRATE work?

ExceleRATE is made up of two assessments, Vitals Mobile (in office) and CORE (on road), which are performed utilizing the Impirica app on approved mobile tablets.


When done independently, Vitals Mobile and CORE provide insight into a driver's fitness for duty based on their cognitive and functional abilities to drive. When completed together, ExceleRATE provides a single, easily understood score that allows decision makers to pinpoint risk and proactively respond.

Vitals Mobile

Driving is a complex task which requires various cognitive functions in order to maintain safety. Across four tablet-based tasks, Vitals Mobile measures 11 out of the 12 cognitive functions identified by the CCMTA as necessary for driving.
