SUCCESS TIPS

How to Prepare for Success

Use these simple study habits to improve your knowledge and increase your confidence before your written and road tests.

01

Read Daily

Spend at least 20–30 minutes reading the handbook every day instead of trying to finish everything in one sitting.

02

Practice Road Signs

Learn traffic signs, pavement markings and right-of-way rules until you can recognize them instantly.

03

Take Notes

Write down important rules, speed limits and safety procedures to make revision easier before your knowledge test.

04

Ask Questions

If you are unsure about any topic, our instructors are always available to explain Manitoba driving rules and best practices.
