HOURS OF SERVICE

Hours of Service Course

Drivers are required to comply with the Federal and Provincial Hours of Service Regulations. Barnala Driver Training Academy provides Hours of Service training to help commercial drivers understand these regulations and apply them correctly in daily operations.

Avoid Penalties

Understanding Hours of Service regulations helps drivers avoid costly fines, violations, and enforcement actions while remaining compliant during every trip.

Maintain License

Following the regulations correctly protects your commercial driver's licence and supports a professional driving record.

Reduced Downtime

Proper trip planning and compliance reduce delays caused by inspections, violations, and unnecessary interruptions.

Work-Life Balance

Hours of Service rules help drivers manage driving time, on-duty hours, and required rest periods for a healthier work schedule.

Proper Rest

Learning how to schedule mandatory rest periods helps reduce fatigue and improves alertness behind the wheel.

Protect Lives

Hours of Service regulations are designed to improve road safety by reducing driver fatigue and preventing accidents.

Prevent Fatigue

Understanding driving limits and rest requirements helps commercial drivers stay focused and operate safely throughout their workday.
