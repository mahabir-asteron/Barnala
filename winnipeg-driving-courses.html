Our Truck Driving & Safety Courses in Winnipeg

Class 1 Driver Training (121.5 Hrs & 244 Hrs)

Learn to operate semi-trailer trucks with confidence. Our Class 1 driver training in Winnipeg prepares you for long-haul, local, and freight transportation jobs. We offer both 121.5-hour and 244-hour training options.

  • Practical in-cab training
  • Air brake integration
  • Road test preparation
  • Real-world highway driving

Class 3 Driver Training

Master heavy-duty straight trucks like dump trucks, delivery vehicles, and service trucks. This course is ideal for those working in construction, logistics, and waste management.

  • Vehicle inspection procedures
  • Defensive driving training
  • Class 3 license Manitoba practice test preparation

Class 5 Driving Lessons

Looking to get your personal driver's license? Our Class 5 driving lessons in Winnipeg are beginner-friendly and taught by certified instructors.

  • GDL preparation
  • Private lessons available
  • Road test prep with mock testing

Air Brake Endorsement

If you plan to drive Class 1 or Class 3 vehicles, you'll need an air brake endorsement. Our air brake course in Winnipeg ensures you're fully prepared for both written and practical endorsement exams.

  • In-depth air brake system theory
  • Hands-on brake adjustment training
  • Certification on completion
